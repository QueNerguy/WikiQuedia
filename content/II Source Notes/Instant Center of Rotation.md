### Instant Center of Rotation
28-01-2025
#### Referencing:
[Wikipedia - Instant center of rotation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instant_centre_of_rotation)

#### Synopsis:
The instant center of rotation (ICR) is a point on an object that doesn't move when subjected to a _pure rotation_. This point can be outside of the rotating object, as seen [here](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/50/Mechanism_analysis.png).
