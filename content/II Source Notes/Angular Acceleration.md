### Angular Acceleration
28-01-2025
#### Referencing:
[Wikipedia - Angular acceleration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angular_acceleration)

#### Synopsis:
Angular acceleration, $\boldsymbol{\alpha}$, or $\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}$ and $\ddot{\boldsymbol{s}}$, measures acceleration around an axis as a rotation, or alternatively, the _rate of change with respect to time_ of [Angular Velocity](Angular%20Velocity.md). An object will always rotate around it's [Instant Center of Rotation](Instant%20Center%20of%20Rotation.md), no matter where the angular acceleration acts, as long as it is subjected to _pure rotation_. Having only _angular_ acceleration acting on a body causes a pure rotation.<br>The angular acceleration on a [rigid body](Modeling%20Rigid%20Body%20Motion) is the same for any point on that body.

- __Unit:__ $\text{[rad/s}^2 \text{]}$
