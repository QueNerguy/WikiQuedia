# Engineering Thermodynamics Learning Overview


### Begin?
1. ___[Calculus I](!%20Calculus%20I%20Learning%20Overview):___ Integration and differentiation, as well as the *total differential*, are used continuously throughout all of the mathematical formulations of thermodynamics, and as such knowledge of all of calculus I is necessary to understand the math of thermodynamics.
2. ___[Calculus II](F!%20Calculus%20II%20Learning%20Overview):___ Partial differentiation and other calculus II concepts are used sporadically to understand the math in thermodynamics.  Understanding calculus isn't a prerequisite to understanding thermodynamics itself per se, but it will play a role in some formulas. Where the math is used a backlink or footnote can be found to refresh the necessary concept from calculus, meaning that not all of calculus II has to be understood to understand thermodynamics.
3. ___[[Introduction to Engineering  Thermodynamics]]:___ An introduction to what thermodynamics is and the scope of the subject as covered here.
