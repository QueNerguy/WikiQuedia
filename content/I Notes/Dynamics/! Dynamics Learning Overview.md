# ! Dynamics Learning Overview
Dynamics is a continuation of statics%%LINKJEEEEE STATICS%%. In contrast to statics, in dynamics it is assumed that movement occurs.


### Background
1. ***[Calculus I](!%20Calculus%20I%20Learning%20Overview):*** Dynamics makes use of calculus to model motion. A good grasp of [differentiation](Differentiation) and [integration](Integration) is imperative to be able to make dynamics computations.
2. ***Differential Calculus:***%%LINKJE%% Dynamics makes use of differential calculus to model motion. *Differential calculus* goes more in-depth on the (non-rigorous) use of differentials, which applies to almost every aspect of dynamics.
3. ***Statics:***%%LINKJE%% The basic concepts of statics are reused in dynamics to also include moving and accelerating objects.
4. ***Linear Algebra:***%%LINKJE%% Vectors and matrices and their operation are commonplace in dynamics computations. They allow us to understand complex systems with single or few equations.

### Part 1 - Kinematics
Kinematics studies *only* the motion of [particles](Modeling%20Particle%20Motion) or [rigid bodies](Modeling%20Rigid%20Body%20Motion).
1. ___[[Modeling Particle Motion]]:___ Particles are the simplest objects to study. This note will lay down the framework for motion of all sorts. 
2. ***[[Modeling Rigid Body Motion]]:*** Rigid bodies are objects that consist of one or multiple particles and are assumed to be non-flexible. Rigid bodies are only modeled if an approximation as a particle isn't accurate enough. As such rigid body motion is a generalization of [particle motion](Modeling%20Particle%20Motion) that applies for all objects that are assumed to be non-flexible.


### Part 2 - Kinetics
Kinetics studies motion as induced by (outside) forces and torques. Solving for these models always includes studying the motion itself and thus always makes use of [kinematics](#Part%201%20-%20Kinematics) to relate *forces* etc. to concrete *displacements*.
