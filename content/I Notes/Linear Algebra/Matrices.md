# Matrices

### Geometrical Interpretation

### Definition


#### Differentiation rules
Differentiation of a matrix $A$ means differentiation of the *elements* in matrix $A$. As such, if we have a matrix filled with constants we know that it's derivative is the zero matrix. <br>In math speak:
$$
A' = \left[ a_{ij}' \right]
$$
The order of matrix multiplication is important, and this is true for derivatives as well. For the derivative of matrices $A$ and $B$ under matrix multiplication we have
$$
\frac{d}{dx}(AB) = \frac{dA}{dx} B + A\frac{d}{dx}B
$$
We thus keep the order of multiplication, even under differentiation.
